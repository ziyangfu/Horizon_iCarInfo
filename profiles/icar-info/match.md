# Vehicle Motion Control and intelligent chassis news profile

Use this profile for technical, industry, product, and regulatory developments in **Vehicle Motion Control (VMC) / Chassis Motion Control (CMC)**, intelligent chassis systems, vehicle dynamics, actuator coordination, and software-defined chassis.

Typical items include:
- **Vehicle Motion Control (VMC / IVDC / VMM)**: Centralized chassis control, control allocation, tire force allocation, multi-actuator coordination, direct yaw moment control (DYC), torque vectoring.
- **Line-Controlled Chassis Actuators**: Steer-by-Wire (SBW), Rear Wheel Steering (RWS/4WS), Brake-by-Wire (EHB/EMB/One-box), wheel slip control, active suspension (CDC, air springs, active roll stabilization, preview suspension).
- **State & Parameter Estimation**: Sideslip angle estimation, vehicle velocity estimation, tire-road friction coefficient estimation, nonlinear tire forces.
- **Advanced Control & Simulation**: Model Predictive Control (MPC), sliding mode control, fault-tolerant chassis control, CarSim/CarMaker/HiL/SiL simulation, AUTOSAR chassis software platforms.
- **Tier-1 Suppliers & OEMs**: Technical announcements, product releases, and mass production dynamic updates from core VMC suppliers (Bosch, ZF, Continental, Nexteer, Mando, Schaeffler, Brembo, Hitachi Astemo, JTEKT, Bethel, Tongyu, NASN, etc.) and OEM chassis engineering divisions.

Do not use this profile for general EV consumer reviews, marketing promotions, sales/delivery figures, price wars/discounts, infotainment/cockpit gadgets, battery chemistry/charging posts, or perception algorithms unrelated to chassis/motion control.
