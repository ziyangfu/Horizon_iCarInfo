# Intelligent vehicle and chassis news profile

Use this profile for technical, industry, academic, and regulatory developments in smart vehicles, autonomous driving AI, intelligent chassis systems, vehicle dynamics, and automotive hardware/software engineering.

Typical items include:
- Line-controlled chassis technology: Steer-by-Wire (SBW), Brake-by-Wire (EHB/EMB), Active/Air/Electromagnetic Suspension, Integrated Vehicle Dynamics Control (VMC/IVDC), Chassis Domain Controller (CDC).
- Autonomous driving and AI: End-to-End AD models, Occupancy Grid, World Models, simulation/closed-loop testing, automotive AI chips and compute platforms (Horizon Journey, NVIDIA Thor, Huawei MDC).
- Academic papers and research breakthroughs: ArXiv, IEEE T-IV, IEEE T-MECH, ICRA, IROS, CVPR papers regarding vehicle control, robotics perception, and chassis dynamics.
- OEM and Tier-1 industry developments: Technical announcements, product releases, ISO 26262/21448 safety standards, C-NCAP/E-NCAP updates from OEMs (Tesla, Huawei, BYD, NIO, XPeng, Li Auto) and Tier-1s (Bosch, Continental, ZF, Bethel, Baolong).

Do not use this profile for general EV consumer reviews, marketing promotions, sales figure PR without technical substance, executive PR quarrels, or non-automotive consumer tech news.
