# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance and technical depth of smart vehicle, autonomous driving AI, and intelligent chassis developments for engineers, researchers, and automotive industry analysts.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Paradigm-shifting line-controlled chassis architectures (e.g. fully decoupled SBW/EMB mass production), revolutionary End-to-End AD models, breakthrough IEEE/ArXiv papers on vehicle control/perception, or major safety/regulatory standards.
- **7-8: High value.** Significant chassis or AD technological advancements, OEM/Tier-1 new tech platform releases, high-quality academic papers, insightful engineering deep-dives, or major open-source automotive updates.
- **5-6: Useful.** Incremental improvements in vehicle software/hardware, moderate technical announcements, useful open-source tools, or industry analysis with solid data.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine model year updates, shallow summaries, or updates dominated by promotional/marketing material.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic content, marketing spam, unverified gossip, or generic PR without engineering substance.

# Evaluation guidance

Assess technical novelty, chassis dynamics/control depth, AI model architecture innovation, engineering feasibility, and industry impact. Prioritize concrete technical details, experimental/test data, and algorithmic or hardware breakthroughs over marketing claims.

Use three to five specific topic tags (e.g., `steer-by-wire`, `brake-by-wire`, `active-suspension`, `end-to-end-ad`, `vehicle-dynamics`, `chassis-domain-controller`).
