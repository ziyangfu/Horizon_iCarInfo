# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the innovative value, technical solution completeness, and patent barrier significance of automotive chassis and AD patent publications.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Breakthrough patent.** High-value core patent with key mechanical/electronic/control redundancy innovation for mass-production wire-controlled chassis or AD systems.
- **7-8: High value.** Important technical solution solving specific failure modes, cost reductions, or control precision in vehicle systems.
- **5-6: Useful.** Standard structural or algorithmic patent with clear practical implementation.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Minor procedural or decorative patent with limited technical barrier.
- **0-2: Noise.** Irrelevant or trivial patent publication.

# Evaluation guidance

Assess applicant, patent claim scope, technical problem solved, and structural/control method novelty.

Use three to five specific topic tags (e.g., `#patent`, `#chassis-control`, `#steer-by-wire`, `#brake-by-wire`, `#fault-tolerance`).
