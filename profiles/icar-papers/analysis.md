# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the academic rigor, technical novelty, and engineering value of research papers in intelligent vehicles, vehicle dynamics, and autonomous driving AI algorithms.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Paradigm-shifting paper introducing novel control/AI methodologies with extensive real-vehicle or closed-loop benchmark validation.
- **7-8: High value.** Solid theoretical contributions, novel algorithmic architectures, or significant performance improvements on standard benchmarks.
- **5-6: Useful.** Incremental improvements on existing models or control methods, solid baseline experiments.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Minor variations with weak baseline comparisons or limited experimental validation.
- **0-2: Noise.** Irrelevant topics, low quality, or invalid formulations.

# Evaluation guidance

Assess mathematical rigor, theoretical novelty, experimental/real-vehicle validation, open-source code/dataset availability, and relevance to intelligent vehicle systems.

Use three to five specific topic tags (e.g., `#world-model`, `#vehicle-dynamics`, `#steer-by-wire`, `#end-to-end-ad`, `#motion-planning`).
