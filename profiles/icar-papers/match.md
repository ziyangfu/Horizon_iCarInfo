# Frontier academic papers profile

Use this profile for academic papers, preprints (ArXiv), journal articles (IEEE T-IV, IEEE T-MECH, etc.), and conference proceedings (ICRA, IROS, CVPR, NeurIPS) related to intelligent vehicles, chassis dynamics, control theory, autonomous driving AI models, and robotics.

Typical items include:
- Vehicle dynamics and control theory: Steer-by-Wire (SBW), Brake-by-Wire (EHB/EMB), Active Suspension, Integrated Vehicle Motion Control (VMC), Model Predictive Control (MPC), sliding mode control, fault-tolerant control.
- Autonomous driving & AI models: End-to-End AD, World Models, Occupancy Networks, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, trajectory prediction, trajectory planning, closed-loop simulation.
- Perception and localization: Point cloud processing, 3D object detection, SLAM, sensor fusion, radar/camera/LiDAR calibration.

Do not use this profile for commercial press releases, marketing product announcements, general news, or non-academic articles.
