# Frontier academic papers profile

Use this profile for academic papers, preprints (ArXiv), journal articles (IEEE T-IV, IEEE T-MECH, Vehicle System Dynamics, etc.), and conference proceedings related to **Vehicle Motion Control (VMC)**, vehicle dynamics, control allocation, state estimation, and intelligent chassis engineering.

Typical items include:
- **Vehicle dynamics and control theory**: Integrated Vehicle Motion Control (VMC/IVDC), control allocation, tire force allocation, Steer-by-Wire (SBW), Brake-by-Wire (EHB/EMB), active suspension, Model Predictive Control (MPC), sliding mode control, fault-tolerant control.
- **State & Parameter Estimation**: Sideslip angle estimation, tire-road friction estimation, tire parameter identification, nonlinear observer design (EKF, UKF, MHE).
- **AI & Data-Driven Dynamics**: Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN), Neural ODE for vehicle dynamics, reinforcement learning for chassis motion control.
- **Motion Planning & Vehicle Control Integration**: Path tracking, trajectory tracking, stabilization near handling limits, closed-loop simulation (CarSim, CarMaker).

Do not use this profile for commercial press releases, marketing product announcements, general news, or non-academic articles. Do not use for pure computer vision, LLM NLP, or battery chemistry papers unrelated to vehicle dynamics.
